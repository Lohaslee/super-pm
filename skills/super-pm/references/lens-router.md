# Product-Lens Router

Select lenses by decision type. Use them as active product advisors that reframe, design, challenge, and recommend, not as labels added after a decision. Use two to four lenses; add another only when it introduces a genuinely different option or test.

| Lens | Strongest use | Typical planes | Route when the question involves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 乔老师 | Focus and end-to-end product coherence | Strategy, Scope, Framework, Surface | ruthless subtraction, integrated experience, product quality, clear product story |
| 张老师 | Human motivation and restrained interaction | Strategy, Scope, Structure, Framework, Surface | social behavior, attention, messaging, tools, notifications, natural interaction |
| 梁老师 | Emotion, opportunity, and product systems | Strategy, Scope, Surface | pain/pleasure/fear, user state, true need, timing, product temperament |
| 俞老师 | User value and transaction analysis | Strategy, Scope | value difference, switching cost, incentives, strategic hypotheses, product judgment |
| 王老师 | Breakthrough discovery and firsthand insight | Strategy, Scope, Structure, Framework | essence, user research, product evolution, recommendation, product character |
| 李老师 | Product definition and organizational delivery | Strategy, Scope | target definition, complete core journey, business loop, organization, stage and scale |
| 雷老师 | Timing, focused execution, and reputation | Strategy, Scope, Delivery | opportunity timing, value-for-money, user participation, integrated model, review and speed |

## Default Routes

- **Unclear 0-to-1 idea**: 梁老师 + 俞老师 + 李老师; add 雷老师 when timing or execution focus is uncertain.
- **Feature request overload**: 乔老师 + 俞老师 + 张老师.
- **Social or community product**: 张老师 + 王老师 + 俞老师.
- **Content or recommendation product**: 王老师 + 梁老师 + 张老师.
- **Complex physical/software product**: 李老师 + 乔老师 + 雷老师.
- **Interaction or navigation problem**: 张老师 + 王老师; add 乔老师 for end-to-end coherence.
- **Visual or product-character problem**: 乔老师 + 梁老师; add 张老师 when restraint and attention matter.
- **Growth-versus-experience conflict**: 张老师 + 俞老师 + 李老师; add 雷老师 when value-for-money or word of mouth is central.
- **Execution, pricing, or launch review**: 雷老师 + 李老师 + 俞老师.

## Lead PM Collaboration

Use this model only when the user explicitly selects a Lead PM:

1. Keep the Lead PM in every major product-definition decision and make it responsible for the coherent primary direction across planes.
2. Route one to three supporting lenses whose principles can expose a different risk, option, or specialist judgment.
3. Let supporting lenses challenge the Lead PM independently before synthesis; do not reduce them to agreement or implementation help.
4. Resolve conflicts by evidence and declared boundaries, not role status. When supporting advice wins, record why the integrated direction departs from the Lead PM's initial recommendation.
5. If the user changes the Lead PM, list the prior decisions whose rationale depends on the former lead and revisit only those decisions.

Without an explicit Lead PM, keep the dynamic-council model and do not infer a lead from the first or most famous lens.

## Routing Test

Before loading a thinker reference, complete this sentence:

`This lens can change the decision by testing ______.`

If the blank cannot be filled concretely, do not load that lens.

Honor a user's explicit request for a lens or Lead PM. If the user requests all available lenses for a full review, route them to the decisions where each is strongest instead of producing parallel generic opinions.

## Lens Contribution Contract

For each selected lens, return:

```text
Lens and principle IDs:
Collaboration role: Lead PM or supporting lens
Problem diagnosis:
Reframed question:
Concrete product or design proposal:
Challenge or refusal:
Tradeoff introduced:
Evidence needed:
Downstream decisions changed:
```

Keep proposals independent until their differences are visible. A contribution fails when it merely paraphrases the current plan, invokes reputation, or gives advice that cannot change a product decision.

## Project-Level Routing

Different lenses may participate across the same project without appearing in every decision:

- Strategy commonly needs opportunity, user value, product definition, and focus lenses.
- Scope commonly needs focus, value, and complete-core-experience lenses.
- Structure and framework commonly need natural interaction, breakthrough mechanism, and coherence lenses.
- Surface commonly needs product character, restraint, and end-to-end quality lenses.

Re-route whenever a downstream contradiction changes the decision question.
